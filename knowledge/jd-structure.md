# Public job descriptions: structure only (2026-11-08)

Purpose: shape the fictional JDs in the mock data. These are structural observations in our own words. No posting text is copied; mock JDs are written from scratch.

## Common sections across postings
1. About the company (stage, product, team size)
2. The role / why it exists
3. What you'll do (responsibilities, often 5–8 bullets)
4. What we're looking for (requirements; usually mixes must-haves and nice-to-haves without labelling which is which)
5. Nice to have
6. Logistics: location/remote, compensation range (often missing), start date, process

Observation that supports P′: postings rarely separate must-have from trainable, rarely state 6/12/18-month outcomes, and rarely explain why the role is open. A JD alone is an insufficient brief; the kickoff and later messages carry the real criteria.

## Role-specific patterns
- **Senior backend (logistics):** production ownership and on-call, Go or similar with Postgres, queues/event systems, reliability/SLOs; experience asks range from 5 to 7+ years; logistics domain listed as a bonus.
- **AI agent engineer:** LLM-based multi-step agents, tool use and integrations, evaluation and guardrails, Python, agent frameworks named as examples; RAG/vector stores often listed; seniority asks vary widely.
- **Founding engineer:** build the first architecture, own the full stack and infrastructure, set engineering practices, help hire, work with founders on product; experience asks vary from new graduates to 5+ years; ambiguity tolerance and ownership emphasised; long hours sometimes stated.
