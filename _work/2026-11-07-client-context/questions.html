The job description is not enough. How does the harness keep the role understanding current?

Source: your message of 2026-11-07 (prompts/client-context/001-user.md). Everything under “What you told us” comes from you. Everything else is a proposal and stays unapproved until you answer.

Fact: clients are SMBs, ≤ 80–100 peopleFact: every client touchpoint is re-analysedFact: sources are simulated in a folderFact: no production deploy

What you told us

  • A job description describes a team, but not what the company expects of that team or where the company is heading. The harness has to reason about both.
  • A kickoff meeting captures current state and expectations at 6, 9, 12 and 18 months.
  • Any message or meeting can change the earlier understanding, including budget, urgency and importance. Some changes are small and some are large. Each one must be re-analysed against what we already know.
  • Client restrictions matter, for example required universities or previous employers.
  • Sources to simulate in a folder: email, calendar, meeting transcripts (tl;dv-style), Slack/Teams, Notion, plus external intel: news, funding, Glassdoor-style reviews, forums, social posts and hiring signals.
  • More information is not better. Dispersed or noisy context degrades results, so relevance has to be filtered. Rule: do not overfill the context window.
  • Results are saved to a simulated ATS/CRM (HubSpot-, Greenhouse-like or internal).
  • In production: raw files go to a file bucket and derived knowledge to a database with RAG embeddings. For this assessment, local files such as JSONL are acceptable.
  • The AI reaches data through a CLI or Node.js tools it can start itself. Setup after cloning from GitHub should be minimal, for example pnpm install and then start.

Proposed reframing of the primary objective

Proposal P′: keep the agency's understanding of a client role accurate and current as client communications and company signals arrive. After each touchpoint, the recruiter sees what changed, why it matters (budget, urgency, must-haves, constraints, horizon), and what to do next. The ATS/CRM record is updated only after the recruiter confirms.

How this relates to the earlier draft P (recruiter calibration): the living role profile becomes the reference against which a recruiter's understanding could later be checked. Question C1 asks which of the two leads.

Example flow

JD → kickoff transcript → role profile v1 → founder email: “board wants profitability, push start date to Q3, cap budget” → delta: urgency ↓, budget ↓, seniority under review, severity material → recruiter confirms → profile v2 → ATS note + task “re-confirm salary band”.

Proposed role-profile fields

Company stage and trajectory · why the role exists now · team today and what it expects · what the company expects of the team · outcomes at 6/9/12/18 months · must-have vs. trainable · client restrictions (with risk flag) · budget / urgency / priority · open unknowns · source citations per field · version and “as of”.
