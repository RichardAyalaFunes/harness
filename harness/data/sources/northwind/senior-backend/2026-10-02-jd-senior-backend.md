# Senior Backend Engineer (Northwind Freight Tech)

## About us
Northwind builds routing and dispatch software for road freight carriers. We are 55 people and closed our Series A in September.

## What you'll do
- Build and run the services that plan and price road freight routes.
- Improve the reliability of our dispatch pipeline.
- Work with product on new carrier integrations.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years building backend services in production.
- Strong Go.
- PostgreSQL under real load.
- Event-driven systems: queues, retries, idempotency.

## Nice to have
- Logistics or routing domain experience.
- Kubernetes.

## Practical
Senior level. Salary €70–80k. Remote within EU time zones. Start as soon as possible.
